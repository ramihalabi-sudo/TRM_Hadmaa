Future real-time monitoring

A production deployment needs a server-side scheduler, manufacturer-specific catalog adapters, source/date verification, deduplication, and email or push delivery. A local HTML file cannot monitor websites while closed.

Manufacturer coverage and methodology

Each record includes a link to an official maker page or official datasheet. The catalog is a researched starting set, not a promise of every detector worldwide. Availability/export conditions must be confirmed with the manufacturer.

What the fields mean

Pixel pitch = center-to-center pixel distance in micrometers; Resolution = physical active array or, for MP-only pages, maker's advertised approximate megapixels; Product type separates an FPA from IDCAs, CMOS chips, complete cameras, and camera cores.

Catalog family only means the manufacturer lists the product but the exact pitch/matrix was not established from the reviewed extract. The app does not fill missing numbers by guessing.

SWIR can include extended SWIR. Some products may cross band boundaries; a dual MWIR/LWIR sensor appears once for each spectral channel with the same official source.
